Questions

Reasonable questions,
unreasonable answers.

Does Maillard One need an internet connection?

For toasting, no. The Neural Browning Engine runs entirely on-device. For firmware updates, personalised insights and the Maillard Morning Brief, yes.

What happens if I put in something that isn’t bread?

Crumb Vision recognises 340 breads and 1,200 non-bread objects. Maillard will decline to toast anything it believes to be a phone, a hand, or a Pop-Tart it considers “a different category of commitment.”

Is my bread data private?

All inference happens on-device. Anonymised crumb telemetry is shared only if you opt in, which you did, by owning a toaster.

Can it burn toast?

In 11 million test slices, the Neural Engine has burnt three. We reviewed them. We have all moved on.

Will it become sentient?

We can neither confirm nor deny. Please see Section 14.2 of the Terms.

What is the return policy?

30-day toast-back guarantee. If you don’t love your toast, send it back. The toaster, not the toast. We have been very clear about this.
